Why Most Enterprise AI Seat Rollouts Turn Into Shelfware

Buying 250 enterprise AI licences and sending an announcement email produces a predictable curve: a two-week curiosity spike, followed by a steep drop to roughly 25 to 35% weekly active usage. Staff do not avoid AI because they dislike saving time. They stop using it because nobody gave them approved workflows for their actual job, nobody clarified which customer data is off-limits, and nobody cleared time for peer clinics.

This kit puts the commercial maths and the operational rollout in one place. Model the real cost of dormant seats, work out how many minutes per week each employee must save after review overhead to pay for their licence, and turn those figures into a staged 90-day rollout plan with a day-60 reclaim loop.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is AI licence shelfware and why does the calculator default to 28% active usage?
AI licence shelfware is the share of paid enterprise AI seats that sit idle or get opened once a month for a quick curiosity prompt. Across unassisted blanket rollouts, weekly active usage commonly stalls between 20% and 35%, meaning two-thirds of the annual bill buys unused capacity. Starting the slider at 28% gives finance and enablement teams a realistic baseline instead of vendor sales-deck optimism.
How are break-even minutes per week per employee calculated?
The calculator converts your monthly seat cost into a weekly figure across 46 working weeks per year (accounting for UK annual leave and bank holidays), then divides that weekly cost by your average fully-loaded hourly rate and multiplies by 60. It also shows the effective break-even minutes required from each active user to carry the dormant seats you are still paying for.
Why should we start with a Champion cohort instead of buying seats for every employee on day one?
Blanket seat rollouts burn cash during the first 60 days while departments still lack approved workflows, prompt templates and data rules. Provisioning a 12% to 15% Champion cohort first lets curious staff prove real workflows, catch compliance snags early and show colleagues working examples before you commit full-company licence spend.
Does the tool send my organisation name, headcount or policy edits to a server?
No. Every calculation, SVG chart, policy edit and Markdown export runs locally in your browser using vanilla JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded to a backend or third-party AI API, and shareable links store your settings strictly inside the URL hash fragment.
Can I use the Acceptable Use One-Pager as formal legal advice?
No. The one-pager is a practical plain-English starting draft written so employees actually read and follow it, rather than ignoring a 40-page PDF. Always run the final version past your legal, data protection and information security leads, especially in FCA, UKGC, SRA or HIPAA-regulated environments.
What makes an adoption KPI anti-vanity?
Vanity metrics like total logins or total prompts count activity without proving value. Anti-vanity KPIs measure Weekly Repeat Workflow Rate, review-adjusted time saved after checking and rework, error or escalation rates, and how fast inactive licences get reclaimed after 30 days of silence.

Key Points
  • Shelfware is a rollout failure, not a software defect. Paying for 250 licences at 28% active usage makes every active user carry 3.6x their own seat cost just to break even.
  • Review-adjusted time matters more than raw generation speed. Saving 30 minutes drafting a document that takes 25 minutes to fact-check only nets 5 minutes. Measure net time after verification.
  • A 30-day inactivity reclaim loop pays for the whole enablement programme. Send a polite 7-day check-in before reclaiming dormant licences and move them straight to waitlisted power users.
  • One Champion per 12 to 15 staff beats top-down training slides. Peers copy a colleague who fixed a real Tuesday afternoon task far faster than they follow a 45-minute webinar.
  • Plain-English traffic-light data rules reduce shadow AI. Pair clear Red/Amber/Green boundaries with a 5-day fast-track route to approve new tools so staff stop pasting work into personal accounts.
  • Zero external dependencies or tracking of inputs. Works offline after load, persists your state in the URL hash when shared, and never sends company figures to any server.
