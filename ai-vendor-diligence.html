Why Stress-Test AI Vendors Before Signing?

        
Most AI vendor sales decks look impressive in a 30-minute demo and fall apart the moment infosec or legal asks three basic questions: where do our prompts go, who owns our fine-tuned workflows on exit, and what happens when your upstream model provider deprecates a snapshot on two weeks' notice. In regulated sectors like iGaming, financial services, and healthcare, one sloppy training clause or missing audit log turns a cheap pilot into a board-level compliance problem.

        
This stress-tester scores up to three competing AI vendors across 25 weighted checks, refuses to let high surface scores hide hard deal-blockers, and includes a local contract red-flag scanner that catches sneaky terms without pasting confidential vendor paperwork into an external LLM. Once you have selected a supplier, document the deployed system inside the RAI Governance Framework.

      


      


        

          
            Frequently Asked Questions
            
          
          

            

              

                
Does any vendor data or pasted contract text leave my browser?

                
No. Every scorecard calculation, SVG chart, Markdown export, and policy clause scan runs locally in your browser using vanilla JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded to a server or sent to a third-party AI model.

              

              

                
Why can a vendor score over 75 and still get a Do Not Sign verdict?

                
Seven of the 25 checks are marked as hard deal-blockers, including contractual training opt-out, zero data retention, audit logs, and exit export rights. Failing a hard deal-blocker caps the verdict at Do Not Sign because strong UX or SSO does not offset losing control of regulated customer data.

              

              

                
How should an AI founder use Founder Mode before enterprise sales calls?

                
Switch to Founder Mode, score your own AI product honestly across all 25 checks, and work through the deal-blocker remediation queue first. Once your controls are verified, copy or print the Enterprise AI Trust & Readiness One-Pager to attach to security questionnaires and proposals.

              

              

                
How does this tool differ from the RAI Governance Framework on JohnB.io?

                
The RAI Governance Framework covers internal AI governance for systems your organisation builds or operates, including risk tiering, system cards, and an AI registry. This stress-tester sits upstream during third-party procurement and vendor selection, testing contracts, commercial exit rights, and technical architecture before you sign.

              

              

                
Is the AI Terms & Policy Red-Flag Scanner legal advice?

                
No. The scanner uses deterministic keyword and phrase rules to spot common procurement traps such as silent model training clauses, weak liability caps, and unilateral model swaps. Treat the flagged clauses as a starting brief for your legal counsel, DPO, or infosec team.
