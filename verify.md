# Verify a Course Certificate

> Public verification page for AI & Machine Learning Course certificates issued by JohnB.io (format: johnb.io/verify/JB-2026-XXXXXX).

- **Canonical URL**: https://johnb.io/verify
- **Markdown URL**: https://johnb.io/verify.md
- **Author**: [John Bowman](https://johnb.io/about)

## How Certificate Verification Works

Every graduate who completes all 35 lessons and passes the final exam of the [AI & Machine Learning Course](https://johnb.io/course/) receives a unique certificate ID in the format `JB-2026-XXXXXX`.

1. Visit `https://johnb.io/verify/JB-2026-XXXXXX` (replacing `JB-2026-XXXXXX` with the holder's certificate ID).
2. The page queries the JohnB.io verification record and displays the graduate's verified name, completion date, exam score, and course curriculum details.
3. If a certificate ID does not exist or has been withdrawn, the page reports "Certificate not found".
