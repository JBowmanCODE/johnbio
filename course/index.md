## Course Curriculum (9 Units, 35 Lessons)

### Unit 1 - Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

1. [What is AI? - History, definitions & the Turing Test](https://johnb.io/course/what-is-ai) (8 min) - Covers AI history from the 1950s Dartmouth Workshop, Alan Turing's 1950 test, AI winters, and why modern AI is pattern matching at scale. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/what-is-ai.md))
2. [Categories of AI - Narrow, General & Super AI](https://johnb.io/course/categories-of-ai) (6 min) - Explains the three tiers of AI capability: Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI), Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), and Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/categories-of-ai.md))
3. [How AI Learns - Supervised, Unsupervised & Reinforcement Learning](https://johnb.io/course/how-ai-learns) (10 min) - The three core learning paradigms that power modern AI systems, with practical examples of labelled training, clustering and reward-driven agents. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/how-ai-learns.md))
4. [AI vs. Augmented Intelligence](https://johnb.io/course/ai-vs-augmented-intelligence) (5 min) - Explores the practical distinction between autonomous AI replacing human tasks and augmented intelligence enhancing human judgment. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/ai-vs-augmented-intelligence.md))

### Unit 2 - The Modern AI Landscape

1. [Traditional AI vs. Generative AI - Architecture differences](https://johnb.io/course/traditional-vs-generative-ai) (9 min) - How rules-based and predictive statistical AI differs from modern generative foundation models that synthesise text, code, audio and images. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/traditional-vs-generative-ai.md))
2. [Machine Learning, Deep Learning & Neural Networks - How they relate](https://johnb.io/course/ml-deep-learning-neural-networks) (10 min) - How classical machine learning, multi-layer neural networks and deep learning fit together and when simpler models outperform deep networks. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/ml-deep-learning-neural-networks.md))
3. [Key AI Subfields - NLP, Computer Vision & Edge AI](https://johnb.io/course/key-ai-subfields) (8 min) - Overview of natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, speech recognition, robotics and recommender systems. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/key-ai-subfields.md))
4. [AI Tools & Platforms - LLMs, Agents, RAG & the 2026 market](https://johnb.io/course/ai-tools-platforms-2026) (10 min) - Survey of the leading foundation models, APIs, cloud ML platforms and developer tools used in production in 2026. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/ai-tools-platforms-2026.md))

### Unit 3 - Ethics, Governance & Responsible AI

1. [Real-World AI Failures & Why Ethics Matter](https://johnb.io/course/ai-failures-ethics) (9 min) - Real-world case studies of AI deployment failures and the ethical governance principles needed to prevent harm. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/ai-failures-ethics.md))
2. [Hallucinations, Bias & Intellectual Property](https://johnb.io/course/ai-hallucinations-bias-ip) (8 min) - Why large language models hallucinate, how training data bias propagates into decisions, and copyright and intellectual property considerations. 3. [AI Governance Frameworks & Enterprise Guidelines](https://johnb.io/course/ai-governance-frameworks) (10 min) - How organisations and regulators govern AI systems under the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and UK AI principles. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/ai-governance-frameworks.md))

### Unit 4 - Python for AI

1. [Python Essentials for AI - Variables, data structures & control flow](https://johnb.io/course/python-essentials-for-ai) (12 min) - Core Python data structures, functions, comprehensions and environment management required for machine learning workflows. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/python-essentials-for-ai.md))
2. [Data Analysis with NumPy & Pandas](https://johnb.io/course/numpy-pandas) (12 min) - Vectorised array operations in NumPy and tabular data cleaning, filtering and aggregation with Pandas. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/numpy-pandas.md))

### Unit 5 - Machine Learning

1. [Linear Regression & Gradient Descent](https://johnb.io/course/linear-regression-gradient-descent) (11 min) - How linear regression models continuous outcomes and how gradient descent iteratively minimises a loss function. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/linear-regression-gradient-descent.md))
2. [Logistic Regression & Classification](https://johnb.io/course/logistic-regression-classification) (10 min) - Binary and multi-class classification using the sigmoid function, log-loss optimisation and decision thresholds. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/logistic-regression-classification.md))
3. [Model Evaluation - Validation, Overfitting & Metrics](https://johnb.io/course/model-evaluation) (11 min) - Measuring model performance with accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, confusion matrices, ROC-AUC and cross-validation. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/model-evaluation.md))

### Unit 6 - Deep Learning & Neural Networks

1. [Biological vs. Artificial Neurons](https://johnb.io/course/biological-vs-artificial-neurons) (7 min) - How biological neurons inspired the mathematical perceptron, weights, biases and activation thresholds. ([.md](https://johnb.io/course/biological-vs-artificial-neurons.md))
2. ## How It Works

1. **AI and machine learning fundamentals.** Units 1 and 2 cover what AI actually is, how it differs from traditional software, the main subfields (machine learning, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, robotics), and the history behind modern AI systems.
2. **Core machine learning concepts.** Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. How models learn from data, how they generalise, and where they fail. Bias-variance tradeoff, overfitting, regularisation, and evaluation metrics explained clearly.
3. **Deep learning and neural networks.** How multi-layer networks work, backpropagation, CNNs for image recognition, RNNs and LSTMs for sequences, and the transformer architecture that powers ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
4. **Python and ML libraries.** NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch - the core toolkit for any ML practitioner. Each lesson focuses on the concepts you need rather than turning into a coding tutorial.
5. **Generative AI, prompt engineering, and RAG.** How LLMs work under the hood, how to write effective prompts, retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning, and the practical differences between the leading models.
6. **Model deployment, MLOps, and AI ethics.** Taking models from notebook to production. CI/CD for ML, monitoring, data drift, responsible AI, bias in training data, governance frameworks, and the EU AI Act.

Yes, completely. All 35 lessons across 9 units are free to read without an account. You only need to sign up to track your progress and earn a certificate of completion.

No prior experience is required. The course starts from the absolute basics - what AI actually is - and builds up to advanced topics like deep learning, NLP, and MLOps. Each lesson links to the next so you can follow the path in order.

### How long does the course take to complete?

That depends on your pace. Most people work through one or two lessons per session. At that rate, you can finish all 35 lessons in a few weeks. There's no time limit - you go at whatever pace suits you.

### What certificate do I get?

Once you complete all 35 lessons and pass the final exam, you can download a certificate of completion from your dashboard. It's a JohnB.io certificate showing you've covered AI fundamentals through to deployment and ethics.

### Can I take individual lessons without doing the whole course?

Yes. Every lesson is a standalone page you can read directly. If you just want to understand one topic - say, how transformers work or what MLOps means - go straight to that lesson. No account needed.

### What topics does the course cover?

The course covers AI and machine learning fundamentals, supervised and unsupervised learning, deep learning and neural networks, NLP and large language models, Python and ML libraries, model evaluation and deployment, generative AI, AI ethics, and MLOps. Full curriculum is listed above.
