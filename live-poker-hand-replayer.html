    Frequently Asked Questions
    What does the Live Poker Hand Replayer do?
    It turns a poker hand you describe into an animated replay on a virtual table. You see every player's name, chip stack and position, then step through each bet, call, raise and fold street by street, right up to the showdown.
    Is the Live Poker Hand Replayer free?
    Yes. Building hands with the form, replaying, sharing and embedding are free with no limits. The AI note parser is also free but capped at 5 hands per day to keep the tool running.
    Can I share a replay with friends?
    Yes. Every replay gets a short share link that's easy to post anywhere, and anyone who opens it sees the same replay. You can also copy an embed code to drop the replay into a forum post, blog or website.
    What happens if the AI can't read my notes?
    You never lose your work. Whatever the AI did manage to understand is loaded into the hand builder form, with a note on what's missing, so you fill in the gaps rather than starting again.
    Which poker games does it support?
    No Limit Hold'em with 2 to 9 players, as a cash game or a tournament hand. Antes and straddles are supported, and all-ins with side pots are worked out automatically.
    Do you store my hands anywhere?
    Only the hand itself, never anything about you. Short links keep the hand's details behind a random code so they stay tiny, while embed codes carry the whole hand inside themselves. There are no accounts, and hands are never linked to who made them.
    How It Works
    1. Enter the hand. Use the builder to set blinds, players, stacks and cards, then tap the action as it happened - the tool only ever offers legal moves. Or paste your written notes and let the AI build the hand for you.
    2. Watch the replay. The hand plays out on the table: chips slide in, streets are dealt, stacks update. Step forward and back through every decision or let it auto-play at 1x, 1.5x or 2x.
    3. See the result. At showdown the tool ranks the hands, names the winner ("two pair, kings and eights") and ships the pot - side pots and split pots included.
    4. Share it. Copy a link that holds the entire hand, or grab the embed code and put the replay in a forum thread or on your own site.
    Key Points
    • Built for live hands. No hand history files needed - if you can describe the hand, you can replay it.
    • Poker-correct. Turn order, minimum raises, all-ins, side pots and split pots all follow the rules automatically.
    • Nothing stored. The whole hand is compressed into the share link - no server, no database, no account.
    • Embeds anywhere. The iframe embed works on forums, blogs and coaching sites.
    • 2 to 9 players. Heads-up to full ring, cash or tournament, with antes and straddles.
