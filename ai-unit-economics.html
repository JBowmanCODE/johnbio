    Why Most AI Cost Spreadsheets Understate Production Spend

    Early prototypes lie about unit economics. A single-turn prompt looks cheap on a slide deck, then real users hold six-turn conversations, agents loop twelve times calling JSON tools, retries eat another 10%, and uncached system prompts get billed on every step. This modeller puts context snowballing, prompt caching, model routing and SaaS ARPU margin maths in one place so founders and engineering leads can spot margin traps before launch.

    Frequently Asked Questions
    Why do multi-turn AI agents cost far more than single-shot LLM calls?
    Stateless APIs require you to send the system prompt and prior conversation or tool history on every turn. In a 10-step agent loop without compaction, turn 10 pays to re-read turns 1 through 9, so input tokens grow roughly with the square of the turn count.
    How does prompt caching work in this unit-economics model?
    Major API providers discount repeated prefix tokens by up to 90% when a request hits a warm cache. This modeller applies your chosen cache hit rate to the static system prompt and accumulated session history prefix, while charging full input rates for fresh RAG chunks and new user turns.
    What AI COGS percentage is healthy for a B2B SaaS product?
    Traditional SaaS targets 75% to 85% gross margins, leaving room for cloud hosting, payment fees and support. If LLM spend alone exceeds 20% to 25% of ARPU, your total gross margin will usually fall below 70% once non-AI infrastructure is included.
    When does self-hosting open-weights models beat paying for APIs?
    Self-hosting only wins once sustained traffic keeps your GPUs busy enough to clear the fixed 24/7 compute floor. Below roughly 35% average utilisation or at low monthly token volume, idle GPU hours make self-hosting more expensive than fast-tier APIs.
    Why are retries and evaluation sampling included separately?
    Production systems rarely run at zero waste. Malformed JSON, tool-call retries and rate-limit backoffs typically add 3% to 15% on top of happy-path token estimates, and automated LLM-as-a-judge monitoring adds another measurable cost slice.
    Does any of my pricing or usage data leave my browser?
    No. Every calculation, chart, Markdown brief and CSV export runs locally in JavaScript. Scenario links encode your numbers in the URL parameters only when you choose to copy and share them.

    Key Points
    • Context growth compounds fast. On a 12-step agent loop, re-sending uncompacted history can triple total session cost compared with a rolling summary window.
    • Output tokens carry a 4x to 5x multiplier. Cutting verbose responses and tightening structured JSON schemas often saves more money than trimming a system prompt.
    • Model routing is the biggest practical lever. Sending 70% to 85% of routine calls to a fast-tier model while reserving frontier models for hard steps typically cuts annualised API spend by 60% to 80%.
    • Prompt caching requires stable prefixes. Changing timestamps or per-user IDs placed at the top of your system prompt break cache hits; keep volatile context at the end.
    • Self-hosting has a utilisation cliff. Dedicated GPUs bill 730 hours a month whether users are active or asleep, so always model realistic off-peak utilisation rather than peak load.
